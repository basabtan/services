Iblis / Satan

Read alongside the sources
Is this passage accurate?
    Your question
    with passage
    The Quran records the command: “Prostrate yourselves to Adam.” They all prostrated except Iblis, who refused.

    can you check if this is accurate?

    ZEAL / SOURCE READING

    Yes. The command and the single refusal are both in the recorded text.1 The refusal is repeated in a shorter form elsewhere, where Iblis is described as proud and counted among the disbelievers.2 Your wording is a fair paraphrase; the text itself does not use the phrase “the command”.

    Your question

    who did he mean here

    ZEAL / SOURCE READING

    “He” is Iblis. The verse names him as the one exception to the prostration.3

    Your question

    do other traditions have this scene?

    ZEAL / SOURCE READING

    A refusal to bow to Adam appears outside the Quran, but not in the same places.

    • IslamicAngels are commanded to prostrate; Iblis alone refuses, citing fire over clay.1
    • ApocryphaSatan refuses Michael’s order to worship Adam as God’s image, and is expelled.4
    • ChristianNo such scene in the canonical Bible. A Syriac tradition records Satan refusing to worship Adam.5
    • JewishNo refusal scene recorded. One midrash has angels nearly hailing Adam before God intervenes.6
